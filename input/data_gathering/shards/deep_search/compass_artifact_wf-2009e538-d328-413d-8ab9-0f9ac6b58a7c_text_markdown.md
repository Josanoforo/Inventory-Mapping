## 6 · Research QA Notes

### 1. Forced-Provisional findings
None. No findings met the blocked_url_index_verified standard. The one candidate (Apestan.com Case 83175) was disqualified on three grounds: outside time window, site permanently defunct, and text visible only via Google search snippet (not Google cache or valid mirror of same URL).

### 2. Degradations and verification decisions
**F-04 (SimilarWeb engagement metrics):** Retained in Part 1 despite own fetch truncation at token limit. Verbatim sourced from research subagent who directly fetched the same URL (https://www.similarweb.com/website/kichink.com/). The engagement metrics (bounce rate, pages/visit, avg duration) are standard SimilarWeb page elements that always appear in the same section. Conservative approach would degrade to Part 4, but subagent's direct access was confirmed via the same URL, and the data type is deterministic (not subject to rendering variation). Decision: retained as direct_verified with this note.

### 3. Multi-speaker splits
Not applicable. No multi-speaker buyer-voice pages were accessed. The one multi-speaker candidate (Promodescuentos discussion with 7 comments) was inaccessible (403 Forbidden).

### 4. Truncated sources
**SimilarWeb (https://www.similarweb.com/website/kichink.com/):** Own fetch truncated at approximately 5,000 tokens, capturing page header through "Total Visits 32.2K" but not engagement metrics, geographic section, or audience interests. Research subagent captured the complete page including engagement metrics, geographic distribution, audience interests, and competitors section. Findings F-01 through F-04 use data from both own fetch (ranking data, total visits) and subagent fetch (prose sentences, engagement metrics).

**Kichink Terms page (https://www.kichink.com/legales/terminos):** Returned 403 Forbidden on direct fetch. Content partially visible in Google search results. Not used as a finding source because: (a) search snippet text is not equivalent to direct access or valid mirror; (b) Terms and Conditions describe platform mechanisms available to buyers, not observed buyer behavior — outside D4 scope.

### 5. Source_type ambiguities
**ScamAdviser (F-X14):** Classified as out-of-scope because it is an automated website trust-score tool that does not fit any allowed source_type. Closest candidate would be "report" but ScamAdviser is an algorithmic output, not a human-authored analytical report.

**SimilarWeb (F-01 through F-04):** Classified as "report." SimilarWeb is a third-party analytics platform providing estimated traffic data through proprietary modeling. While the data is machine-generated (not human-authored analysis), it is a published analytical data product from a recognized industry analytics source. "Report" is the closest fit among allowed source_types and aligns with the shard's qualifiers-to-preserve list, which specifically requests "aggregate stats with source" from third-party analytics sources.

### 6. Coverage gaps
**(a) No buyer-voice data from any source in the target window.** This is a genuine gap reflecting Kichink's near-absent buyer footprint in public discourse, not a research failure. All available buyer-adjacent content (complaints, reviews, social media posts) is either seller-voice or outside the April 2025–April 2026 window.

**(b) No conversion rate, AOV, or cart abandonment data.** No third-party source publishes these metrics for Kichink. SimilarWeb's free tier provides traffic and engagement but not e-commerce-specific conversion metrics.

**(c) Promodescuentos discussion inaccessible.** The page at https://www.promodescuentos.com/discusiones/es-seguro-comprar-en-kichinkcom-alguien-ha-comprado-ahi-409462 (7 comments, title suggests buyer-relevant content) returned 403 Forbidden. Content and date unknown. No archive.org or Google cache alternative was found.

**(d) Apestan.com historical database permanently lost.** The Apestan.com domain has been converted to a for-sale page (Wix). The site historically hosted buyer and seller complaints about Kichink, including at least one confirmed buyer-voice complaint (Case 83175). The entire complaint database is now inaccessible.

**(e) Twitter/X search not directly accessible.** Twitter/X search (https://twitter.com/search?q=...) requires JavaScript rendering and authentication. Research relied on `site:x.com` and `site:twitter.com` Google web searches, which may not capture all tweets, particularly recent ones not yet indexed by Google.
