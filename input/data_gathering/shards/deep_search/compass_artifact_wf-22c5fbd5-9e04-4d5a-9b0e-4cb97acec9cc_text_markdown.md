# Domestika × D2: Seller experience and workarounds — Data gathering report

**This report captures 35+ distinct findings from 20+ unique instructor voices** documenting the Domestika seller/instructor experience between 2022 and early 2026. The dominant narrative is a dramatic before/after arc: instructors describe a world-class production experience and fair compensation before 2023, followed by systematic royalty degradation, non-payment, and communication blackouts starting mid-2025. The most substantive source is Class Central's November 2025 investigation interviewing 17 instructors directly. Reddit, Trustpilot, and YouTube yielded near-zero instructor-side content — instructor voices concentrate on LinkedIn, Instagram, personal blogs, and investigative journalism.

---

## Finding index

Each finding below follows the format: one seller × one experience, result, workaround, or frustration. Findings are grouped by priority category.
