# Domestika's fee structure: what the platform officially discloses (and what it doesn't)

**Domestika does not publicly disclose teacher commission percentages, revenue share splits, advance fees, royalty rates, minimum withdrawal amounts, or payment schedules on any official page.** The only specific commission figures published on domestika.org relate to the affiliate program (40%/20%/$2). Teacher compensation terms appear to be handled through individual, confidential contracts. What follows is an exhaustive catalog of every fee, commission, pricing, and payment policy finding from official domestika.org sources, organized as discrete units of observation.

---
