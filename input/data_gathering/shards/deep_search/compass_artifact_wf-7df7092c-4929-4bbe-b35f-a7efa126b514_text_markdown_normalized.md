# ENVATO × D4 — BUYER BEHAVIOR: DATA GATHERING CATALOG

---

## SEARCH DECOMPOSITION

**SD-01: Discovery channels.** How do buyers find Envato products? (Google search, direct visit, referral, social media, YouTube, ChatGPT.) Searched: SimilarWeb traffic sources, Semrush referral data, Trustpilot/forum mentions of discovery, blog accounts of finding Envato.

**SD-02: Purchase experience.** What do buyers report about checkout, payment methods, pricing transparency, and billing on Envato? Searched: Trustpilot envato.com reviews mentioning pricing/payment, Envato forums buyer discussion re payment issues, Sitejabber reviews, blogs about pricing.

**SD-03: Refund and dispute behavior.** How do buyers seek refunds or dispute purchases? What outcomes do they report? Searched: Trustpilot/Sitejabber refund complaints, Envato forums refund threads, Reddit envato refund.

**SD-04: Subscription behavior (Envato Elements).** How do buyers use the Elements subscription? (Short-term, project-based, annual vs monthly, cancellation, AI features.) Searched: Envato forums subscription threads, Trustpilot subscription complaints, blog reviews of Elements subscription value.

**SD-05: Aggregate behavioral metrics.** Traffic, session duration, bounce rate, download volumes, subscriber counts. Searched: SimilarWeb, Semrush, Shutterstock SEC filings (Envato operating metrics), Envato Unpacked 2025.

**SD-06: Cross-platform comparison (buyer voice).** When buyers compare Envato to other platforms as buyers, what do they report? Searched: Trustpilot mentions of competitors, Reddit comparisons, blog comparison reviews.

**SD-07: Post-purchase experience.** What do buyers report after purchase? (Product quality, support, licensing, product removal.) Searched: Trustpilot/Sitejabber post-purchase complaints, forum support threads, blog reviews of product quality.

**SD-08: AI generation behavior.** How do buyers use and react to Envato's AI tools and generation limits (February 2026 change)? Searched: Trustpilot AI-related reviews, blog coverage of AI tier changes, Envato Unpacked AI statistics.

**SD-09: Envato sub-brand differentiation.** Do buyers behave differently across ThemeForest, CodeCanyon, Envato Elements, Placeit? Searched: Trustpilot themeforest.net reviews, SimilarWeb themeforest.net and elements.envato.com separately, forum threads mentioning specific sub-brands.
