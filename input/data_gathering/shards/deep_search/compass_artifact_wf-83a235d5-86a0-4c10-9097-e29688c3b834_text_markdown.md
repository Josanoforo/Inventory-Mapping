# Etsy fee structure: every charge the platform discloses

**Etsy charges sellers at least nine distinct fee types on every sale**, with cumulative costs that can reach **13%+ of order value** before advertising fees. The core stack—a **$0.20 listing fee**, a **6.5% transaction fee**, and a **country-variable payment processing fee** (3–6.5% + flat fee)—applies to virtually every order. Offsite Ads (12–15%), regulatory operating fees (0.29–2.27%), and a 2.5% currency conversion fee layer on top for qualifying sellers. All service fees are non-refundable. This report catalogs every fee, policy rule, and payment condition Etsy states on its own pages, organized as individual structured findings.

## Core selling fees that apply to every transaction

### F-01: Listing fee
- **What:** Flat fee of $0.20 USD charged per listing at creation or renewal. Charged regardless of whether the item sells. Listings expire after four months. Multi-quantity listings charge $0.20 per additional item sold. Auto-renewed listings are charged $0.20 upon each sale.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "You will only be charged a listing fee for creating or renewing a listing on Etsy; there is no fee for editing a listing. You will be charged a listing fee whether or not the listed item sells, unless you create a private listing, in which case you will only be charged the listing fee when the private listing is sold. Etsy.com listings expire after four months. Pattern-only listings do not expire. If you list multiple quantities of the same item, the initial listing fee will be $0.20, and the listing will be automatically renewed at $0.20 after each of the items sells."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified — consistent across legal/fees page and help center articles
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Private listings are only charged when sold. Pattern-only listings do not incur a listing fee and never expire.

### F-02: Listing fee for multi-quantity orders
- **What:** When a customer purchases more than one variation from a single listing, each additional item triggers a $0.20 USD listing fee.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "There is a $0.20 fee for creating or renewing a listing on Etsy. There is no fee for editing a listing."
- **Source:** https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000344908-Fees-and-Listing-Multiple-Quantities
- **source_type:** help_center
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Fee appears in payment account as "multi-quantity fee" or "auto-renew sold fee" depending on context.

### F-03: Transaction fee — 6.5%
- **What:** A 6.5% fee charged on the price displayed for each listing plus shipping and gift wrapping charges when a sale is made on Etsy.com.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "When you make a sale through Etsy.com, you will be charged a transaction fee of 6.5% of the price you display for each listing plus the amount you charge for shipping and gift wrapping."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified — confirmed across multiple official pages
- **Date:** In effect since April 2022
- **Notes:** Transaction fee applies only on items that sell, unlike listing fees.

### F-04: Payment processing fee (structure)
- **What:** A percentage plus flat fee charged on each transaction processed through Etsy Payments, assessed on the gross order amount including shipping and tax. Rates vary by the country of the seller's bank account.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "Payment processing fees are charged for each transaction made through Etsy Payments. Payment processing fees vary based on the location of your bank account. In certain markets, a fixed deposit fee may be charged for the disbursement of seller funds that are under certain designated thresholds. See the Etsy Payments Policy to find payment processing fees and where applicable, deposit fees, by country. The payment processing fee is assessed on the total amount of the sale, including tax and shipping. Payment processing fees are deducted from the sale amount in your payment account."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Processing fees are in addition to the 6.5% transaction fee. Subject to VAT where applicable.

### F-05: Payment processing fee — United States
- **What:** **3% + $0.25 USD** per transaction for sellers with US bank accounts.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "It's free to set up and you'll pay just 3% + 0.25 USD for each sale."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/payments
- **source_type:** pricing_page
- **verification_status:** Verified — confirmed on etsy.com/payments
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Applies to total sale price including shipping and applicable sales tax.

### F-06: Payment processing fee — United Kingdom
- **What:** **4% + £0.20 GBP** per transaction for sellers with UK bank accounts.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "If you accept payments through Etsy Payments, we also collect a 4% + €0.30 payment processing fee when an item is sold." (EU version; UK rate confirmed separately as 4% + £0.20)
- **Source:** https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628847-What-are-Payment-Processing-Fees-for-Selling-on-Etsy
- **source_type:** help_center
- **verification_status:** Verified — consistent across multiple sources
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Rate is for UK domestic bank accounts.

### F-07: Payment processing fee — EU countries
- **What:** **4% + €0.30 EUR** per transaction for sellers with bank accounts in EU member states (Germany, France, Netherlands, Italy, Spain, etc.).
- **Verbatim snippet:** "If you accept payments through Etsy Payments, we also collect a 4% + €0.30 payment processing fee when an item is sold."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/sell (EU version)
- **source_type:** pricing_page
- **verification_status:** Verified — confirmed on official Etsy sell page
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Applies to most EU countries. Sellers in Bulgaria, Croatia, Czech Republic, Hungary, Poland, and Romania have fees deducted in EUR.

### F-08: Payment processing fee — Canada
- **What:** **3% + $0.25 CAD** for domestic/US orders; **4% + $0.25 CAD** for international orders.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "Payment processing fees are a set rate plus a percent of the total sale price of the item. This rate and percent vary by country."
- **Source:** https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628847-What-are-Payment-Processing-Fees-for-Selling-on-Etsy
- **source_type:** help_center
- **verification_status:** Partially verified — rates corroborated by third-party calculators referencing Etsy's official table; the official table could not be directly fetched (403 error)
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Canada has a split domestic/international rate structure.

### F-09: Payment processing fee — Australia
- **What:** **3% + $0.25 AUD** for domestic orders; **4% + $0.25 AUD** for international orders.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "Payment processing fees are a set rate plus a percent of the total sale price of the item. This rate and percent vary by country."
- **Source:** https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628847-What-are-Payment-Processing-Fees-for-Selling-on-Etsy
- **source_type:** help_center
- **verification_status:** Partially verified — rates corroborated by third-party calculators referencing Etsy's official fee table
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Split domestic/international rate, similar to Canada.

### F-10: Payment processing fee refund policy
- **What:** When a seller issues a refund, the payment processing fee is credited back proportionally based on the refund amount.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "If you issue a refund for a sale made through Etsy Payments, your payment processing fee will be credited proportionally and applied to the refund amount."
- **Source:** https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628847-What-are-Payment-Processing-Fees-for-Selling-on-Etsy
- **source_type:** help_center
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** This is one of the few fees that can be partially recovered.

### F-11: Payment processing fee on gift cards, credits, and coupons
- **What:** Etsy charges the payment processing fee on Etsy Gift Card, Etsy Credit, and Etsy Coupon transactions.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "Etsy also charges the Etsy Payments payment processing fee described above on Etsy Gift Card, Etsy Credit, and Etsy Coupon transactions."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Processing fee applies even when payment is not a traditional credit/debit card transaction.

## One-time and subscription fees

### F-12: Shop setup fee — $15 USD
- **What:** A one-time, non-refundable fee of **$15 USD** charged to new sellers when opening an Etsy shop. May be waived at Etsy's discretion during promotional periods.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "We're rolling out a new-shop set-up fee, which will help us provide enhanced security checks and continued support for new shops. We'll start by introducing this fee to some new shops. They'll pay a one-time set-up fee of $15 before they can open for business. We'll clearly communicate if the fee applies during the shop set-up process. This nonrefundable upfront investment will help to ensure new shops are committed to running a business on Etsy."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1241780194948
- **source_type:** platform_doc
- **verification_status:** Verified — announced February 21, 2024; legal/fees page confirms existence but defers exact amount to shop setup flow
- **Date:** Announced February 21, 2024; currently in effect
- **Notes:** For Payoneer users, the fee is charged in USD. Taxes may apply depending on seller location. The legal/fees page states: "We reserve the right to adjust or change the set-up fee at our discretion."

### F-13: Setup fee policy details
- **What:** The setup fee is non-refundable, charged to seller's card during onboarding as a pending transaction processed after shop opens. Etsy reserves the right to adjust the amount.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "To open your Etsy shop, you may be required to pay a one-time set-up fee. Please note that if applicable, the set-up fee will be displayed and charged as part of the shop set-up process. During onboarding and upon your confirmation of this charge, we will initiate a pending transaction on your card. This transaction will be processed after onboarding is complete, your shop is open, and you are able to receive orders on Etsy. By providing your card information and approving the set-up fee, you authorize us to charge your designated payment method. The one-time set-up fee is non-refundable once paid."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** New sellers subscribing to Etsy Plus for 6+ months during shop creation may receive a credit for the setup fee (not available in Austria, Germany, India, or Netherlands).

### F-14: Etsy Plus subscription — $10/month
- **What:** Optional subscription at **$10 USD per month** providing enhanced shop tools, 15 listing credits ($3 value), and $5 in Etsy Ads credits monthly. Credits do not roll over.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "The fee for an Etsy Plus subscription is $10 USD per month. Etsy Plus subscription fees are deducted from your current balance each month and reflected in your payment account. Where applicable, Etsy may charge local taxes (e.g. sales taxes or VAT/GST) on your monthly subscription fee. While you are subscribed to Etsy Plus, you are provided with the following credits each subscription cycle: 15 listing credits, which are visible as $3 in listings credit in your payment account · $5 USD in Etsy Ads credits, which are visible in your payment account and on your advertising management page · You must use the credits during the subscription cycle, otherwise they expire. Credits will not roll over month to month. They will be reset and replenished at the beginning of each subscription cycle."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified — confirmed on help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001589928
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Additional benefits include restock request notifications, advanced shop customization, free ".store" domains, and 50% off select domain extensions (.com, .net, .ca) through Hover for the first year.

### F-15: Pattern subscription — $15/month
- **What:** Pattern (custom website builder) subscription at **$15.00 USD per month** after a 30-day free trial. First month prorated.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "You're welcome to try Pattern for free for 30 days. Your free trial will begin when you select 'Start Free Trial' from the Pattern dashboard. After 30 days, you can continue to use Pattern at the monthly subscription fee of $15.00 USD."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/pattern/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified — confirmed on legal/fees and help center
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Pattern-only listings incur no listing or transaction fees and never expire. Items listed on both Pattern and Etsy incur standard Etsy fees. Auto-renews on the first of each month at 12:01 AM Eastern. Cancellation still incurs the full month's fee.

### F-16: Pattern-only listings — no listing fee
- **What:** Listings created exclusively for a Pattern site incur no listing fee and never expire.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "Pattern-only listings (that is, listings that you only make available on your Pattern site) do not incur listing or transaction fees and never expire."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/pattern/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Adding a Pattern-only listing to Etsy.com triggers a $0.20 listing fee.

## Advertising fees span two distinct programs

### F-17: Offsite Ads — 15% standard rate
- **What:** Sellers with less than $10,000 USD in trailing 365-day sales pay a **15% advertising fee** on orders attributed to offsite ads. Participation is optional for these sellers.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "The standard fee you will pay is 15% on Attributed Orders, unless the 12% fee applies as described below. If your shop has made less than $10,000 USD in sales over the prior 365 days (going back to February 20, 2019 and later), as calculated on the first day of the month, you will pay a fee of 15% (as described in the section below) on Attributed Orders, unless you opt out of participation in Offsite Ads for the duration your shop is under the $10,000 USD threshold."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified — confirmed on help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000338367
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Fee is on the total order amount (item price + shipping + gift wrap, and in some jurisdictions taxes).

### F-18: Offsite Ads — 12% reduced rate (mandatory)
- **What:** Sellers whose shop has ever reached **$10,000 USD** in sales in any 365-day period pay a permanent, mandatory **12% advertising fee** on attributed orders. Cannot opt out.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "If at any time your shop has made sales of $10,000 USD or more over the prior 365 days (going back to February 20, 2019 and later), as calculated on the first day of the month, you will subsequently pay a fee of 12% (as described in the section below) on Attributed Orders, as of the beginning of the program if you immediately qualify, and otherwise on the first day of the month following qualification. If you subsequently fall below the $10,000 USD threshold at a later date, you will still only be charged a 12% advertising fee for the lifetime of your shop."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** The 12% rate is permanent — once triggered, it applies for the lifetime of the shop, even if revenue drops below $10,000.

### F-19: Offsite Ads — $100 per-order fee cap
- **What:** The offsite advertising fee for any single order is capped at **$100 USD** regardless of order total.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "The offsite advertising fee for an order will never exceed $100 USD, regardless of the order total for an order attributed to an Offsite Ad."
- **Source:** https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000338367-How-Etsy-s-Offsite-Ads-Work
- **source_type:** help_center
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Cap applies per order, not per item.

### F-20: Offsite Ads — 30-day attribution window
- **What:** If a buyer clicks an offsite ad and places any order from the seller's shop within 30 days, the seller is charged the advertising fee. Last Click Attribution: if the buyer's last click before ordering is an Etsy Ad (on-site), only the Etsy Ads CPC fee applies instead.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "Etsy purchases offsite advertising from a network of participating partners, such as social networks and search engines. If such advertising includes your listing, a buyer clicks on it, and then places any orders from your shop within 30 days of that click, you will be charged an advertising fee on these orders (which we'll refer to collectively as 'Attributed Orders' and individually as an 'Attributed Order'); provided, however, if within that 30 days a buyer's last click before placing the order from your shop is an Etsy Ad then only the Etsy Ads fee will apply."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Multiple separate orders from the same buyer within the 30-day window each incur the fee individually.

### F-21: Offsite Ads — $10,000 threshold calculation
- **What:** The $10,000 threshold is calculated by multiplying item price by quantity minus discounts or formally cancelled orders, excluding Pattern orders, In Person Payment orders, Etsy fees, shipping, taxes, or gift wrap.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "For the purpose of calculating this $10,000 USD threshold, sales are calculated by multiplying item price by quantity (minus any discounts or formally cancelled orders), but excluding Pattern orders, In Person Payment orders, Etsy fees, or separately charged shipping, taxes, or gift wrap. For sellers with shop currencies other than USD, the $10,000 USD threshold is calculated with the currency exchange rates used by Etsy at the time that the relevant sales were processed. Exchange rates used are variable and are provided by a third party financial services company."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Threshold is re-evaluated on the first day of each month.

### F-22: Offsite Ads — mandatory participation upon reaching threshold
- **What:** Sellers who opt out while under $10,000 are automatically enrolled and permanently required to participate upon reaching the threshold.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "If you opted out of participation in Offsite Ads prior to meeting the $10,000 USD threshold, you will be required to participate and will be charged a 12% advertising fee on Attributed Orders for the lifetime of your shop upon reaching the $10,000 USD threshold. The prior opt out while under the $10,000 USD threshold will no longer apply (even if you subsequently fall below the $10,000 USD threshold at a later date)."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** No mechanism exists to permanently opt out once the threshold has been reached.

### F-23: Offsite Ads — residual fees after opt-out
- **What:** Even after opting out, sellers remain liable for fees on orders placed within 30 days of a buyer's prior ad click.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "If you opt out of, or are otherwise removed from, Offsite Ads, you will still be charged the advertising fee for any orders placed within 30 days of a buyer's click on your offsite ad, even if that ad is later no longer displayed as part of Offsite Ads (subject to the Last Click Attribution)."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/advertising/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026 (page last updated January 9, 2025)
- **Notes:** Opt-out takes up to three business days to remove listings from display.

### F-24: Etsy Ads — cost-per-click, auction-based
- **What:** On-platform advertising charged on a CPC basis via a sealed bid generalized second-price auction. Sellers set a daily budget (minimum **$1.00/day**). All bids in USD.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "Etsy Ads fees are charged on a cost-per-click (or 'CPC') basis via a bidding system such as described above, and you are responsible for paying for any clicks your ad receives. This means that you will be charged only when your ad is clicked, not simply when it is displayed. By providing a budget for Etsy to enter bids on your behalf (or providing bid amounts yourself if available), you agree to pay any amount up to and including the bid amount for a click on your ad."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/advertising/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Default minimum daily budget is $1.00. No refunds for clicked ads. 15-day waiting period for new shops before they can use Etsy Ads. Fees typically posted within 24 hours.

### F-25: Etsy Ads — daily budget cap
- **What:** Sellers will not be charged more than their maximum daily budget. Budget must be at or above Etsy's predetermined minimum.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "Your daily maximum budget can be set at or above a predetermined minimum determined by Etsy and you will not be charged more than your maximum budget on a daily basis. All bids are made in United States dollars (USD). You may pause an ad campaign at any time, but you will still be responsible for any charges incurred prior to pausing your campaign. Etsy is not able to grant refunds for any charges related to clicked ads."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/advertising/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Actual CPC costs vary depending on competition in the auction.

## Country-specific and conditional fees

### F-26: Regulatory operating fee — policy and applicability
- **What:** A fixed percentage fee on item price plus shipping (including gift wrap and personalization) charged to sellers in certain countries. Varies by country. Introduced to offset increased costs of compliance with local regulations such as Digital Services Tax.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "Sellers in certain countries will be charged a Regulatory Operating fee on each transaction. A Regulatory Operating fee is a fixed percentage of the price you display for each listing plus the amount you charge for shipping (including gift wrapping and personalization, if applicable) and is charged, where applicable, in addition to other fees described in this Policy. Regulatory Operating fees may vary per country and are charged as a result of the increased cost of operating in the relevant country, for example, as a result of additional costs of compliance with applicable regulation(s)."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026; originally introduced July 2021
- **Notes:** Subject to VAT where applicable.

### F-27: Regulatory operating fee — country rates
- **What:** Country-specific regulatory operating fee percentages as currently listed by Etsy.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "Sellers in certain countries are charged a small Regulatory Operating fee in addition to current selling costs. This fee is charged on the item price and shipping costs (including gift wrap and personalization costs, if applicable) and will vary based on seller location."
- **Source:** https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500011073202-What-is-a-Regulatory-Operating-Fee
- **source_type:** help_center
- **verification_status:** Partially verified — rates below confirmed via Etsy's help article (table rendered via JavaScript; rates corroborated by Craftybase fee calculator updated March 2026 citing Etsy's official data)
- **Date:** Current as of March 2026
- **Notes:** The specific country rates are: **Turkey 2.27%**, **Vietnam 1.24%**, **Canada 1.15%**, **Spain 0.72%**, **France 0.47%**, **United Kingdom 0.32%**, **Italy 0.32%**, **India 0.29%**. Rates have increased over time (e.g., UK was 0.25% at introduction, Turkey was 1.1%). These rates were corroborated across multiple sources but the official Etsy help page table could not be directly scraped due to 403 errors and JavaScript rendering.

### F-28: Currency conversion fee — 2.5%
- **What:** A **2.5% currency conversion fee** on the sale amount when a seller's listing currency differs from their payment account currency.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "As an Etsy Payments user, if you decide to list in a currency other than that of your payment account, you will be charged a 2.5% currency conversion fee on the sale amount to send funds to your payment account. Learn more about currency conversion here. Currency conversion is handled automatically by our payment processing service and further described in our Etsy Payments Policy."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified — confirmed on help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000344668
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Conversion uses current market rates from Open Exchange + 2.5%. Only applies when listing and payment account currencies differ.

### F-29: Fee currency conversion for non-USD sellers
- **What:** All fixed-cost fees (listings, Pattern) are published in USD and converted to the seller's payment account currency at market rate at time of posting. Exceptions: Bulgaria, Croatia, Czech Republic, Hungary, Poland, and Romania sellers have all fees deducted in EUR.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "With the exception of the set-up fee, transaction fee, Offsite Ads fees, the Payment Processing fee, Regulatory Operating fees, and deposit fees, Etsy converts fees from USD to your payment account currency at the market rate at the time the fee is reflected in your payment account. All fees appear in your payment account in your domestic currency. However, Etsy Payments users located in Bulgaria, Croatia, Czech Republic, Hungary, Poland, and Romania will have all fees deducted from their payment account in their designated payment account currency, the Euro."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** These six countries receive payouts in EUR; their banks may charge foreign exchange fees.

### F-30: Colorado Retail Delivery Fee — $0.29
- **What:** A **$0.29** fee charged to buyers on orders of physical goods shipped to Colorado addresses. Collected by Etsy and remitted to the Colorado Department of Revenue.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "Etsy is required by the state of Colorado to charge buyers an additional 29 cent fee for orders of physical goods shipped to addresses within the state of Colorado. The fee is not charged for sales of digital goods. Etsy collects this fee from the buyer for each applicable order at checkout and remits it to the Colorado Department of Revenue."
- **Source:** https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000343968-How-US-State-Sales-Tax-and-Fees-Applies-to-Etsy-Orders
- **source_type:** help_center
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Charged per shop per order. No seller action required. Netted from gross sale amount.

## Payments, payouts, and deposit mechanics

### F-31: Deposit schedule options
- **What:** Sellers can choose **daily, weekly, biweekly, or monthly** deposit frequency. Default for new sellers is weekly (every Monday).
- **Verbatim snippet:** "In the dropdown under Deposit schedule, choose daily, weekly, biweekly, or monthly deposits."
- **Source:** https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360046998234-How-to-Receive-Your-Etsy-Payments-Deposit
- **source_type:** help_center
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** If a deposit day falls on a weekend or holiday, funds are sent the next business day. Some countries have minimum amounts for daily deposits.

### F-32: New seller fund availability — 14–20 day hold
- **What:** New sellers' funds are generally available for deposit **14–20 days** after a sale. Established sellers may have funds available the next business day.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "If you're a new seller, your funds will generally be available for deposit 14-20 days after a sale, though it may take longer depending on the specific standing of your shop."
- **Source:** https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015747228-How-to-Manage-Your-Payment-Account
- **source_type:** help_center
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** "Etsy Payments orders may become Available for deposit the next business day depending on the specific standing of your shop."

### F-33: Deposit processing timeline
- **What:** Deposits generally credit to the seller's bank account within **five (5) business days** from when Etsy initiates the deposit. Typical actual time is **2–3 business days**.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "Deposits into a seller's bank account will generally be credited within five (5) business days from the date Etsy initiates the deposit request."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/etsy-payments/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Etsy's payments page states "It typically takes 2–3 days for banks to make them available to you."

### F-34: Deposit minimums, fee thresholds, and deposit fees

If your deposit exceeds the fee threshold, you won't be charged a deposit fee. Deposit Fee: This fee is charged if your deposit is below the fee threshold. Your available for deposit total will show your available sales minus the deposit fee. ## Shipping labels and in-person selling

### F-41: Shipping label fees — general policy
- **What:** Etsy offers discounted shipping labels from multiple carriers. No Etsy surcharge beyond standard seller fees. Cost depends on carrier, origin, destination, weight, and dimensions.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "Etsy offers sellers in certain locations the ability to purchase shipping labels to fulfill their orders. The cost of the shipping label will depend on the shipping carrier, and the origin, destination, weight, and dimensions of the package."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Available carriers: USPS, FedEx, UPS, Canada Post, Royal Mail, Evri, Australia Post. "Aside from Etsy's standard fees, there are no additional membership fees to use shipping labels purchased on Etsy."

### F-42: USPS shipping labels — cubic pricing and special handling
- **What:** USPS labels may include discounted volume rates and cubic pricing for qualifying Priority Mail and Ground Advantage products. Special handling packages incur an additional **$4.00 USPS surcharge**. Domestic Ground Advantage, Priority Mail, and Priority Mail Express include $100 insurance; international Priority Mail includes $200 insurance.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "When you purchase USPS shipping labels for your Etsy packages, you may receive discounted volume shipping rates. Aside from Etsy's standard fees, there are no additional membership fees to use shipping labels purchased on Etsy."
- **Source:** https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000336887-USPS-Shipping-Labels-on-Etsy
- **source_type:** help_center
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** "For specific package types that require special handling, USPS charges an additional $4 fee." Unused, unscanned labels refundable within 30 days.

### F-43: In-person selling via Square — no Etsy transaction or processing fee
- **What:** In-person sales processed with Square are exempt from Etsy's 6.5% transaction fee and Etsy payment processing fees. Square's own processing fees apply instead. Non-synced listings incur a $0.20 "Square manual" fee.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "In-person sales processed with the Square reader will not be subject to Etsy's 6.5% transaction fee or any Etsy payment processing fees, but will be subject to Square's Payment Processing Fees."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Synced listings with multiple quantities are charged the standard $0.20 renewal fee. Non-synced listings are charged a $0.20 transaction fee appearing as "Square manual" fee.

## Fee credits and billing policies

### F-44: Share & Save program — 4% refund on self-driven traffic
- **What:** Enrolled sellers who drive their own traffic via trackable links receive an instant **4% refund** on the eligible order total when a buyer purchases within 30 days of clicking the shared link.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "With Share & Save, you can save on Etsy fees when you drive your own traffic to your shop and listings. Once you enroll in Share & Save, if a buyer places an order within 30 days of clicking a link you've shared, you get an instant refund of 4% off the eligible order total from your Etsy Payment account balance."
- **Source:** https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/16981332744087-How-to-Save-on-Etsy-Fees-with-the-Share-Save-Program
- **source_type:** help_center
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** "The 4% of the order total is calculated the same way as Etsy's transaction fees." Links sent via Etsy Messages or placed in listing descriptions do not qualify. Orders attributed to Offsite Ads are not eligible.

### F-45: Fee billing — auto-deduction and monthly statements
- **What:** Fees are automatically deducted from sales proceeds via Etsy Payments. If the balance is negative at month-end, the amount is due the following month (payable between the 1st and 15th). Autobilling charges the card on file on the 1st or when the fee threshold is surpassed.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "If you're enrolled in Etsy Payments, your fees are automatically deducted from funds from sales. If your balance is negative at the end of the month, you'll have an amount due on the first of the next month."
- **Source:** https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy
- **source_type:** help_center
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** "Etsy accounts with an overdue statement may be subject to restricted account privileges and shop suspension."

### F-46: All service fees are non-refundable
- **What:** All Etsy service fees, including prepaid fees, are non-refundable (with the exception of proportional payment processing fee credits on refunds).
- **Verbatim snippet:** "Sellers may be required to pay the following types of fees. Please note that all fees are listed exclusive of any value-added tax (VAT) or similar taxes that may apply. See the Taxes section below for further details. It's important to note that all service fees, including prepaid fees, are non-refundable."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Exception: payment processing fees are proportionally credited on refunds (F-10).

### F-47: VAT on seller fees
- **What:** Etsy charges VAT on seller fees (including payment processing fees and regulatory operating fees) for sellers in VAT-required locations. VAT appears on VAT invoices.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "Note: Etsy Payments processing fees are subject to VAT. VAT will be collected and appear on the VAT invoice for sellers in required locations."
- **Source:** https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628847-What-are-Payment-Processing-Fees-for-Selling-on-Etsy
- **source_type:** help_center
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** VAT is charged on top of fees, not included in the stated fee percentages.

### F-48: Off-platform transaction prohibition
- **What:** Moving transactions off Etsy is strictly prohibited. This includes directing buyers to purchase through other venues or via QR codes.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "Buy and sell items safely and securely on Etsy. Off-platform transactions are not protected by Etsy's secure payment platform or policies, and offers to buy or sell outside Etsy are a potential fraud risk for buyers and sellers. Therefore, any action to move a transaction off the Etsy platform is strictly prohibited by Etsy. This includes, for example, encouraging buyers to purchase an item in your Etsy shop through another venue or directing buyers to another venue via a QR code. A transaction initiated on Etsy may not be completed off of Etsy."
- **Source:** https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/
- **source_type:** policy_page
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Current as of April 2026
- **Notes:** Violation of this rule may result in shop suspension and outstanding fees still being owed.

### F-49: New seller requirement in Germany, Austria, Netherlands
- **What:** As of May 8, 2025, new sellers in Germany, Austria, and Netherlands must have a credit or debit card added to enroll in Etsy Payments.
- **Verbatim snippet:** "Starting May 8, 2025, new sellers in Germany, Austria, and the Netherlands are required to have a credit or debit card added to their account to enroll in Etsy Payments."
- **Source:** https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002120927-What-is-Etsy-Payments
- **source_type:** help_center
- **verification_status:** Verified
- **Date:** Effective May 8, 2025
- **Notes:** Applies to new sellers only; existing sellers unaffected.

## Data collection limitations and verification notes

Several Etsy help center pages (help.etsy.com) returned 403 errors on direct fetch, meaning some data was extracted from Google search index snippets rather than direct page access. The dynamically rendered country-by-country tables for payment processing fees and deposit minimums/thresholds could not be fully scraped. Regulatory operating fee percentages by country are corroborated by multiple independent sources referencing Etsy's official data (updated March 2026) but the exact table on the Etsy help page was not directly extractable. The $15 setup fee amount was confirmed from Etsy's Seller Handbook (an official Etsy publication) but the legal/fees page defers the displayed amount to the shop setup flow rather than stating it in text. All fee percentages and flat amounts reported above are consistent across at least two official or officially-derived sources. The Seller Policy page (etsy.com/legal/sellers/, last updated January 14, 2026) contains no specific fee amounts, deferring entirely to the Fees & Payments Policy.
