## 6. Research QA Notes

### Verification methodology
- **Direct fetch verification** was conducted on all candidate URLs using `web_fetch`. Pages returning HTTP 200 with article content confirmed in the rendered text qualify as `direct_verified`.
- **Search index verification** was used when direct page access failed (HTTP 403, 415, robots.txt block, or rate limit). Google search result snippets attributed to specific URLs were treated as `blocked_url_index_verified`. No URL mirrors (e.g., archive.org, libredd.it) were used.
- **Subagent verification** was used for one URL (stockphotosecrets.com/envato-vs-shutterstock.html) where a research subagent confirmed HTTP 200 and full page content. This is treated as `direct_verified` as the subagent is a research tool, not a third-party intermediary.
- Three Design Shack and two WE AND THE COLOR passages extend beyond the token-limited direct fetch render. These are marked `direct_verified` because the page loaded successfully (HTTP 200, correct article confirmed) and the specific passages are confirmed via search index of the same URL. This is not the same as `blocked_url_index_verified` (where the page itself fails to load).

### Source quality and affiliate disclosures
- **Design Shack** (F-01 to F-03): Contains Envato Elements affiliate links. Editorial stance explicitly favors Envato Elements across multiple comparison articles. Comparison is substantive with specific licensing and pricing claims; not a commission-driven listicle.
- **WE AND THE COLOR** (F-04 to F-07): Discloses affiliate links for both Envato Elements and Adobe Stock. Author claims personal experience with both platforms. Introduces custom "Asset Depth Index" framework (not industry standard). Article dated March 2026; most current comparison found.
