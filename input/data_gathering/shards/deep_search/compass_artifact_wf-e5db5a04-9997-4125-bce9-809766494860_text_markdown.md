## Part 3 — Pattern Candidates (sealed)

---

**PC-01**
Multiple findings describe Kichink's commission-only, zero-upfront-cost model as the platform's primary differentiating attribute when compared to subscription-based alternatives. This description appears in self-positioning statements (F-01, F-02, F-P01, F-P04), third-party articles (F-P04, F-P06), and seller accounts (F-06). The commission rate cited varies across sources and time periods: 3.8%–7.5% in 2013–2017 sources, 9.5% in circa-2020 sources, and 15% on the current kichink.com/crea-tu-tienda page.

**PC-02**
Across sources, Kichink is recurrently classified as a marketplace (comparable to Mercado Libre or Amazon) rather than as a standalone ecommerce store builder (comparable to Shopify or WooCommerce). This classification appears in third-party critical reviews (F-04), Shopify partner content (F-P03), forum discussions (F-X05), and commenter exchanges. Sources that make this classification tend to also state that Kichink lacks customization, SEO capability, and scalability features present in Shopify and WooCommerce.

**PC-03**
Third-party sources that compare Kichink to Shopify recurrently describe Kichink as suited for beginners and small businesses, with Shopify positioned as more appropriate for business growth and scaling. This description appears in Terrabionic (F-P03), Marketing4eCommerce (F-X07), Dinero en Imagen (F-X06), and Angel de Cuir (F-P06). The pattern holds across both pro-Shopify and neutral sources.
