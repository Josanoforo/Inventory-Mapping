# Coverage Gaps

Gaps in the corpus identified during scanning that limit the reading of tension patterns.

---

## Perspectiva del comprador ausente en el corpus

- Gap ID: GAP-001
- Signal IDs that create the expectation: SC-R1-034 (Gumroad refund policy article referencing buyer chargeback behavior), SC-R1-038 (Discover mobile app sales final policy referencing buyer refund requests to Apple/Google)
- Description: No card in the corpus documents buyer motivations, buyer conversion behavior, buyer satisfaction, or the buyer decision-making process when acquiring products on Gumroad. Buyer-side actions (chargeback requests, refund requests) are documented only from the platform or seller vantage point.
- Why it limits reading of the inventory: Tension patterns involving refunds (TC-016), chargebacks (TC-016), and discovery (TC-004, TC-011, TC-019) rely on buyer behavior that can only be inferred from seller and platform accounts. The absence makes it impossible to determine whether chargebacks result from buyer fraud, product dissatisfaction, or other causes.

## Cobertura de plataformas alternativas ausente en el corpus

- Gap ID: GAP-002
- Signal IDs that create the expectation: SC-R1-061 (seller described temporarily switching to Gumroad while Lemon Squeezy approval was pending), SC-R1-041 (seller described intent to switch platforms due to dissatisfaction with Gumroad changes)
- Description: No card documents the terms, fees, policies, or seller experiences of Lemon Squeezy, Payhip, or other platforms named or implied in the corpus. The mention of competitors appears only as entities referenced in platform-switching behavior.
- Why it limits reading of the inventory: Tension patterns about platform control (TC-017, TC-021, TC-023) and fee structures (TC-007) cannot be evaluated against alternatives. The corpus cannot establish whether the mechanisms documented in Gumroad are unusual or standard in the industry.

## Datos post-compra ausentes en el corpus

- Gap ID: GAP-003
- Signal IDs that create the expectation: SC-R1-022 (ToS: Gumroad facilitates delivery upon purchase), SC-R1-003 (features page: memberships give customers access to content library while subscribed)
- Description: No card documents what occurs after delivery from the buyer's perspective: product consumption, satisfaction, repeat purchase rates, membership retention, or return rates. The corpus documents the delivery mechanism and membership features but not post-delivery dynamics.
- Why it limits reading of the inventory: Tension patterns involving refunds and chargebacks (TC-016) and membership economics (GAP-007) are incomplete without data on post-purchase outcomes. The absence makes it impossible to establish whether refund rates are correlated with product quality or delivery failures.

## Experiencia de vendedores no angloparlantes ausente en el corpus

- Gap ID: GAP-004
- Signal IDs that create the expectation: SC-R1-027 (help center: 160+ countries can accept payments), SC-R1-019 (ToS: all transactions settle in USD regardless of listed currency)
- Description: No card documents seller experiences from non-English-language markets (Spanish-speaking, Asia-Pacific, or other geographies). All seller-perspective blog and forum cards in the corpus are from English-language sources. The USD settlement and payout restrictions documented in FRIC-002 (TC-012) and CONT-004 (TC-005) would have distinct effects on these populations.
- Why it limits reading of the inventory: Tension patterns about geographic payout gaps (TC-005, TC-008, TC-012, TC-022) are documented only from the platform side. The affected seller populations are not represented in the corpus, making the practical impact of payout restrictions unverifiable from seller accounts.

## Ingresos en rangos intermedios sub-representados en el corpus

- Gap ID: GAP-005
- Signal IDs that create the expectation: SC-R1-056 (seller reported $139.96 total in three years), SC-R1-050 (seller reported $61,411 in 2025), SC-R1-039 (blog author reported $3,271 in first six months)
- Description: Seller income reports in the corpus cluster at extremes: low outcomes (under $200) and high outcomes (over $15,000). The intermediate range ($500–$15,000) is represented by only SC-R1-039 as a single card. Two or more independent cards with outcomes in this range are absent.
- Why it limits reading of the inventory: Tension pattern TC-006 (asimetría distributiva de resultados) cannot establish whether the distribution is truly bimodal or whether the intermediate range is simply under-represented in the corpus. The shape of the distribution between the poles is not documented with sufficient density to support claims about the overall seller income distribution.

## Experiencia de vendedores con tipos de producto específicos ausente en el corpus

- Gap ID: GAP-006
- Signal IDs that create the expectation: SC-R1-065 (Gumroad homepage Discover section features category tags including notion template, mockup, brushes, font), SC-R1-072 (help center: most common items are e-books, films, music, software, tutorials)
- Description: No seller-side card specifically documents experience selling templates, Notion products, AI prompts, spreadsheets, or planners — product types associated with digital product trends in the 2024–2026 period. The corpus documents platform support for these categories but no seller experience with them.
- Why it limits reading of the inventory: Tension patterns about discoverability (TC-004, TC-011, TC-019) and fee impact (TC-007) cannot be evaluated for these product types. Whether the first-sale Discover threshold affects template and prompt sellers differently from e-book sellers is not documentable from the current corpus.

## Dinámica de membresías y suscriptores ausente en el corpus

- Gap ID: GAP-007
- Signal IDs that create the expectation: SC-R1-003 (features page: memberships give customers access to content library while subscribed), SC-R1-075 (help center: membership tiers support varying access levels and add-on services)
- Description: No card documents membership retention, subscriber churn, failed renewal billing, or cancellation dynamics. The corpus documents Gumroad's membership features but not any membership performance outcomes. The fee structure for recurring billing on membership sales is also absent from the corpus.
- Why it limits reading of the inventory: Tension patterns involving platform control (TC-017, TC-021) and seller income distribution (TC-006) cannot be evaluated for the membership revenue model. Whether the mechanisms documented for one-time sales (refunds, chargebacks, fee structures) apply identically to recurring membership billing is not documentable from the current corpus.
